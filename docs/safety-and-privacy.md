# Safety and privacy

This prototype is for education and research only. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, triage system, or treatment recommender. It cannot replace a licensed clinician or formal diagnostic imaging. Do not use it to make decisions about an individual patient.

The 3D diagram is illustrative and schematic. Its color shows an estimated patient-level vessel label probability, not a physical lesion or location. Color bands are visual aids and are not clinical thresholds. A risk probability from this model is not necessarily calibrated.

The app is configured for local development. Do not expose it to the public internet or enter identifiable patient data. De-identify data where appropriate, follow the dataset license and institutional policy, protect model/data files, and review third-party package and font network use before deployment. `data/` and `models/` are excluded from version control by `.gitignore`.
